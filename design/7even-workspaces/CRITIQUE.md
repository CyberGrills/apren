### Five changes for Round 2

1. **Demonstrate the readiness hand-off using actual persisted data.** Show a blocked listing with its exact next action, the save confirmation, then the updated check row and next action. Use a small check/status change and brief 160ms emphasis only after successful persistence; reduced motion should produce the same immediate written result. Do not introduce an optimistic success state or decorative object. If the proposed hand-off is not implemented, mark it unimplemented and score honestly.
2. **Make phone staff sign-in immediate.** On `admin-login-phone`, reduce the introductory display to about 32px and tighten its vertical padding so the email field begins within roughly the first 280px and the submit action is reachable without a full screen of introductory copy. Preserve the existing logo and staff-only explanation.
3. **Expose intentional table scrolling on phones.** In business reservations and all admin tables, show a short “Scroll to see payment and actions” cue when overflow exists, and provide a focusable, labeled scroll region or a labeled row/detail treatment. Show a phone screenshot with a populated row and its action/payment columns; do not hide those fields to fit.
4. **Shorten overview-only repetition.** Keep the full audit/search destinations, but show about five latest entries in the overview and a labeled link to the complete activity history. Render empty exceptions/applications as compact calm status rows, preserving the full detail for nonempty queues. The phone overview should prioritize operational work rather than ten repeated audit events.
5. **Tighten scope language and refresh complete evidence.** On business check/status labels, identify that eligibility is for current unfiltered search rather than promising visibility for every date/location query. Keep the timestamp and the existing explanation that booking checks run again. Supply normalized phone admin, inventory, pending-business and relevant modal/scroll screenshots plus current scans, so small-type or clipping claims are based on the final CSS.

No request here asks for dark mode, an Apple-style wrapper, a new font, removal of safety gates, made-up inventory, or a change to money movement.

**Not done by the stop rule in critique.md.** Signature is below 3, and only seven of the twelve design lines reach 4. These scores describe rendered design evidence, not production correctness, security or release approval.

### Five possible refinements toward exceptional craft

These are optional future refinements, not new release blockers or requests to undo the selected direction:

1. In a separate populated operating-day review, verify that the next real arrival and its permitted action can lead when work is due, rather than keeping readiness dominant once setup is complete.
2. If user testing shows lost row identity during horizontal scrolling, test a sticky reference column or a record detail view. Preserve every amount, delivery state and action.
3. Test the readiness receipt on a short phone with a long listing name and several blockers, including large text, to confirm the next-step control remains easy to reach through intentional dialog scrolling.
4. Observe actual hotel and lounge operators using the pending, draft and ready states. Refine any wording they misunderstand rather than decorating the screens to chase a score.
5. Review a populated admin exception queue with realistic long business names and simultaneous issues. The empty/healthy visual state is reviewed here; unusual exception density should be judged with evidence.
